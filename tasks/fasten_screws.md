## Description

Identify the colors of the nuts and fixed bolts, and install each nut on the bolt of the same color. First seat the nut correctly on the threads, then screw it down while keeping it upright and aligned with the bolt. For each pair, screw the nut down until the top of the bolt’s bounding box is more than 9 mm above the bottom of the nut’s bounding box. Prevent it from tilting, falling off, or merely resting on the top of the bolt. Check that every color pairing is correct and every nut is securely seated, and complete any unfinished installation. Fully open both grippers and return both arms to their initial poses.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** No nut is fastened onto its matching bolt.
- **20 points:** Any one nut is upright, aligned with its matching bolt, rotated onto the bolt to the required depth, and the gripper is open.
- **50 points:** Any two matching nut-bolt pairs are fastened correctly.
- **100 points:** All three nut-bolt pairs are fastened, the gripper is open, and the robot returns to origin.
