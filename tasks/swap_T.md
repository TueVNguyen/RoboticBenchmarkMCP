## Description

Observe and remember the initial position and orientation of both T-shaped blocks, then exchange them so that each block occupies the other's original pose. Match both the location and the direction of the stem and crossbar rather than merely exchanging approximate areas. Keep the same original upper face facing upward throughout the exchange; do not flip either block over. After release, check that both blocks remain in their correct positions and orientations, and correct any displacement or rotation. Fully open both grippers and return both arms to their initial poses.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** The two T blocks are not swapped with the required pose accuracy.
- **100 points:** The two T-shaped blocks swap their original xy positions, their orientations match the swapped targets, and the robot returns to origin.
