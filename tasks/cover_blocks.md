## Description

Observe and remember the color of the block at every position, then cover all blocks from left to right with inverted cups. Once all blocks are covered, recall the color beneath each cup and uncover the blocks in the order red, green, then blue. Keep the cups inverted and avoid moving the blocks or changing the association between colors and positions. Confirm the correct object at each stage before proceeding. Fully open both grippers and return both arms to their initial poses.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** No block-covering step is completed.
- **5 points:** All three blocks are covered by cups from left to right.
- **15 points:** After all blocks are covered, the red block is uncovered first while the green and blue blocks remain covered.
- **30 points:** The red and green blocks are uncovered in order while the blue block remains covered.
- **100 points:** All three blocks are first covered from left to right, then uncovered in red, green, and blue order; cup orientation remains valid and the robot returns to origin.
