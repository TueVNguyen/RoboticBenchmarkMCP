## Description

Hang every mug in the scene on the rack by its handle, one mug at a time. Each supporting branch must pass through the handle so that the rack bears the mug's weight, rather than the mug merely leaning against the rack or remaining held by a gripper. Confirm that each mug stays securely suspended after release before proceeding to the next, and correct any slipping, detachment, or interference between mugs. Release all mugs, fully open both grippers and return both arms to their initial poses. hang_mugs_random uses the same description.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** No mug is hung on the mug rack.
- **15 points:** Any one mug is hung on the mug rack and the gripper is open.
- **40 points:** Any two mugs are hung on the mug rack.
- **100 points:** All three mugs are hung on the rack and the robot returns to origin.
