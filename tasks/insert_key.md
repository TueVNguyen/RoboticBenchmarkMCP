## Description

Insert the key into the lock and turn it clockwise after confirming that it is fully seated. Turn the key to an orientation approximately 60 degrees clockwise from the keyhole's reference orientation when viewed from above. Keep the key properly oriented and inside the keyhole throughout insertion and turning, avoiding slipping out, tilting, or turning only above the opening. Correct any misalignment before continuing. Finish with the key fully inserted and remaining in its turned position. Do not withdraw it. Returning both arms to their initial poses is not required.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** The key is not lifted.
- **15 points:** The key is lifted at least 5 cm.
- **100 points:** The key is inserted deep enough into the slot, aligned closely in x/y, kept upright, and successfully turned after insertion.
