## Description

Confirm that the stand actually supports the headphones rather than the headphones merely leaning against it, and that the closed laptop's bottom is fully seated inside its rack. Check whether placing either object has disturbed the other, and correct any tilting or displacement. Ensure that both objects remain stably placed after release, with the headphones securely supported and the laptop standing firmly in its rack, without slipping, rocking, or tipping. Release both objects, fully open both grippers and return both arms to their initial poses. store_laptop_and_headphones_random uses the same description.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** Neither the headphones nor the laptop is stored correctly.
- **20 points:** The headphones are placed on the stand: the headband bridge is close to the support cradle and aligned, with the gripper open.
- **80 points:** The headphones are correctly placed on the stand, and the laptop is closed, vertically inserted into the laptop rack, and within the required range.
- **100 points:** Both the headphones and laptop conditions are satisfied and the robot returns to origin.
