## Description

Pour all of the liquid from the bottle into the designated cup, keeping the liquid flowing into the cup and minimizing spills onto the table or outside the cup. Observe the transfer as it proceeds, avoid hitting the rim or tipping the cup, and correct any stream that misses the opening before continuing. Once the transfer is complete and the liquid in the cup has settled, bring the bottle upright. Finish with the bottle upright. Returning both arms to their initial poses is not required. pour_liquid_into_cup_random uses the same description.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** The liquid is not successfully poured into the cup.
- **100 points:** The cup contains the required amount of liquid and the bottle is upright.
