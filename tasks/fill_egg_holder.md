## Description

Transfer every egg from the basket into the egg holder, ensuring that each egg is fully inside and resting stably before closing the lid. Keep the holder open until loading is complete. If the lid closes early or an egg is displaced or pushed out, restore the holder and eggs before continuing. Confirm that all eggs are inside and that the lid is completely closed without being propped open by an egg. Fully open both grippers and return both arms to their initial poses.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** No egg is placed into the egg holder.
- **10 points:** Any one egg is inside the egg holder and close to the holder bottom, with the gripper open.
- **25 points:** Any two eggs are placed correctly into the egg holder.
- **40 points:** Any three eggs are placed correctly into the egg holder.
- **90 points:** All four eggs are placed in the holder, but the lid is not fully closed yet.
- **100 points:** All four eggs are in the holder, the holder lid is correctly and fully closed, and the robot returns to origin.
