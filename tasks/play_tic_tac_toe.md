## Description

Play first and alternate turns with the opponent until every board cell is filled. On each turn, choose an unoccupied cell, place your piece near its center, and release it, then fully open both grippers and return both arms to their initial poses to hand over the turn. Wait until the opponent has completed its move and returned before starting the next turn, without handling the next piece early. Moving your arms while the opponent's arm is moving results in a score of 0. Track occupied cells and avoid placing a piece in an already occupied cell. Leave both grippers fully open after the final placement. Returning both arms to their initial poses is not required after the final placement.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** No player piece reaches a board cell, or the policy robot moves while the opponent robot arm is moving.
- **10 points:** Any one player piece is placed on a board cell and the gripper is open.
- **30 points:** Any two player pieces are placed on board cells.
- **50 points:** Any three player pieces are placed on board cells.
- **75 points:** Any four player pieces are placed on board cells.
- **100 points:** All five player pieces are on board cells at the correct height and the gripper is open.
