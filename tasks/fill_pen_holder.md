## Description

Place every pen in the scene into the pen holder, one at a time. For the black pen with a white conical nose and the slim white pen, the writing tip is the small exposed dark point; place that tip downward. For the black-and-gold pen, identify the capped writing end by its gold pocket clip; keep this end upward and place the opposite plain, closed barrel tail downward. For each colored highlighter, the capped writing end is the shorter, slightly tapered section separated from the long barrel by a visible seam; keep this capped end upward and the flat barrel tail downward. Check that each pen rests inside the holder rather than remaining above the opening, lying across the rim, or falling outside, and correct any wrong orientation or unstable placement. Finish with the holder upright on the table, release it, fully open both grippers and return both arms to their initial poses.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** No pen is correctly inserted into the pen holder.
- **10 points:** Any one pen is correctly inserted tip-up into the upright pen holder.
- **25 points:** Any two pens are correctly inserted into the pen holder.
- **40 points:** Any three pens are correctly inserted into the pen holder.
- **90 points:** All four pens are inserted into the upright pen holder, but the robot has not returned to origin yet.
- **100 points:** All four pens are inserted, the pen holder is upright, and the robot returns to origin.
