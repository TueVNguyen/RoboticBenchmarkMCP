## Description

Read the number cards and remember the required press count for each red button. Read each digit exactly as displayed in its normal upright orientation; do not rotate, flip, or reinterpret a number card from another direction, and leave both cards in their original positions and orientations. Complete the required presses on the left red button, then press the blue button to confirm that group. Next complete the required presses on the middle red button and confirm again with the blue button. Every press must include a full depression and complete release; count complete cycles and avoid missed presses, extra presses, or repeated confirmations. Fully open both grippers and return both arms to their initial poses after completing the entire sequence.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** The required press counts or confirm sequence is not completed exactly.
- **100 points:** Button `0` is pressed exactly the number shown by `num0`, the blue confirm button is pressed, button `1` is pressed exactly the number shown by `num1`, the blue confirm button is pressed again, and the robot returns to origin.
