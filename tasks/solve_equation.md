## Description

Read the equation in the current scene, determine the missing number or operator, and select the candidate that makes the equation correct. Use the equation actually displayed rather than assuming a fixed answer. Place the correct piece at the center of the answer pad with the proper orientation so that the completed equation is clearly readable. In the final arrangement, every digit and mathematical symbol must lie flat and follow the normal upright reading direction of the existing equation. Correct any initial skew; do not leave a digit or symbol skewed, sideways, or upside down, even when rotation leaves its appearance unchanged. After release, check for displacement or rotation and correct it. Fully open both grippers and return both arms to their initial poses.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** The missing equation piece is not placed correctly.
- **100 points:** The correct missing number/operator piece is placed on the missing mat and the robot returns to origin.
