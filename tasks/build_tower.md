## Description

Assemble the complete tower from bottom to top: place the large board on the white supports standing on the table, add the remaining white supports on the large board to carry the smaller board, then add the thin top board and green triangular block. For each of the two pairs of white supports—the pair beneath the large board and the pair supporting the smaller board—use one support that started on the left side of the table and one that started on the right. The white supports must bear weight on their broad faces while preserving their original upper faces. Center every layer over its supports, align the boards' long edges, and keep the triangular block's bottom edge parallel to the top board's long edge. Let each layer stabilize before continuing and correct any misalignment or tilting. Release all pieces, fully open both grippers and return both arms to their initial poses.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** No tower layer is completed.
- **10 points:** The lower board is placed across two upright supports, remains stable and centered on them, and the gripper is open.
- **30 points:** The middle board is placed on the completed lower layer, stays centered over it, and all lower and middle pieces remain upright.
- **100 points:** The full tower is completed with the top pieces, all eight pieces remain upright, each layer stays centered over the layer below it, the gripper is open, and the robot returns to origin.
