## Description

Wait for the opponent to discard a Mahjong tile and return its arm to its initial pose before beginning any manipulation. Identify the discard and find the matching triplet among your tiles. Turn only that matching group face-up to declare the kong, leaving all other tiles standing and undisturbed. Then draw the replacement tile from the end of the wall and stand it upright at the far right of all your tiles, flush against the adjacent standing tile. Match the face direction and top-to-bottom orientation of the neighboring standing tiles. Check that the declared group and replacement tile are in their correct states. Leave both grippers fully open. Returning both arms to their initial poses is not required.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** The kong declaration state is not completed.
- **100 points:** The three matching Mahjong tiles are pushed down, the non-matching tiles remain upright on the table, the target tile is correctly grasped and placed at the required position, the gripper is open, and no invalid robot motion is detected.
