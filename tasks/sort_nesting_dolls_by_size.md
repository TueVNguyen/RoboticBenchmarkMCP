## Description

Compare the sizes of all nesting dolls in the current scene and arrange them in a row from smallest on the left to largest on the right. Use their actual sizes rather than assuming fixed positions or a predetermined order. Keep every doll upright, leave clear separation between neighbors, and align the entire row consistently. Keep the left-to-right distance between the centers of every pair of neighboring dolls greater than 3 cm, and keep the front-to-back distance between the frontmost and rearmost doll centers less than 3.5 cm. Correct any doll that is out of order, tilted, or displaced after release. Once all dolls are correctly arranged and stable, fully open both grippers and return both arms to their initial poses. sort_nesting_dolls_by_size_random uses the same description.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** The dolls are not arranged in the required size order.
- **100 points:** All five nesting dolls stand in a one horizontal straight row, are ordered from left to right from smallest to largest, and the robot returns to origin.
