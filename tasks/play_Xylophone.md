## Description

Use the mallet to strike every xylophone key in sequence from left to right. Between consecutive strikes, raise the mallet by more than 2.5 cm relative to its height at the preceding strike. The mallet head must actually contact the intended key for each strike and leave that key before the next strike, avoiding dragging across keys, missing keys, or merging adjacent strikes. Check the mallet head's actual state and correct any displacement so that every strike in the complete sequence is distinct. Finish after striking all keys. Returning both arms to their initial poses is not required.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** The full left-to-right strike sequence is not completed.
- **100 points:** The mallet tip hits all xylophone keys from left to right, and after each hit the mallet is lifted by at least 0.025 m.
