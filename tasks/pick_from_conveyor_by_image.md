## Description

Identify the target object from the image on the display board, first raise the basket by 8 cm from its initial height and keep it level, then take the matching object from the conveyor and place it into the raised basket. Use the image and the objects actually present rather than selecting a nonmatching object. Confirm that the target has been released and rests completely inside the basket rather than on the rim or outside. Finish with the basket still raised and level. Returning both arms to their initial poses is not required.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** The target object is not placed into the lifted basket.
- **100 points:** The image-specified target object is in the basket and lifted at least 8 cm.
