## Description

First fold both sleeves inward across the torso so that each cuff rests near the opposite underarm, keeping the fabric flat and untwisted. Confirm that the sleeves remain tucked in after release, then fold the lower half of the garment upward toward the shoulders, aligning the hem with the shoulder line and keeping both sleeves inside. Check and correct any incomplete, loose, or misaligned folds. Release the garment, fully open both grippers and return both arms to their initial poses. fold_clothes_random uses the same description.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** The sleeve-fold condition is not reached.
- **20 points:** When the gripper opens, both sleeves are folded inward close to the opposite chest points.
- **100 points:** Both sleeves are folded inward, the hems are close to the shoulder points in x/y, the hem line is aligned with the shoulder line within the angle threshold, and the robot returns to origin.
