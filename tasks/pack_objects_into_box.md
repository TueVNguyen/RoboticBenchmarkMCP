## Description

Pack the toy car, hammer, toothbrush, and shoe into the box. Keep the box upright, with its long edges running from left to right across the table. Place each object lengthwise, parallel to the box's long edges. Orient the toy car with its front pointing left and its rear pointing right, the hammer with its head on the left and its handle on the right, the toothbrush with its brush head on the left and its handle on the right, and the shoe with its toe pointing left and its heel pointing right. Plan the packing order according to the objects' sizes and the available space in the box, leaving room for the remaining objects. Every object must fit entirely inside the box and rest on the box bottom or another stable supporting surface. No object may protrude outside the box or rest unstably. Once all objects are correctly positioned, oriented, and supported, release all objects, fully open both grippers and return both arms to their initial poses. pack_objects_into_box_random uses the same description.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** No object is placed correctly into the box.
- **10 points:** Any one object is inside the box with its front side facing left, the box is aligned, and the gripper is open.
- **25 points:** Any two objects are placed correctly into the box.
- **50 points:** Any three objects are placed correctly into the box.
- **100 points:** All four objects are placed in the aligned box with correct facing direction, and the robot returns to origin.
