## Description

Insert all tubes individually into available openings in the rack, keeping each tube upright and fully seated. Check that the tube actually enters the opening rather than stopping at the mouth, leaning against the rack, or becoming stuck at an angle. After release, confirm that the rack supports the tube stably, and correct any slipping, tilting, or incomplete insertion. Complete every tube, fully open both grippers and return both arms to their initial poses.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** No tube is inserted into a slot.
- **20 points:** Any one tube is inside the slot, inserted to the required depth, upright, and the gripper is open.
- **40 points:** Any two tubes are inserted correctly.
- **100 points:** All three tubes are inserted correctly and the robot returns to origin.
