## Description

Place bread slices upright into the toaster, one at a time, until every slot contains a fully seated slice. Prevent slices from slipping, falling over, or disturbing neighboring slices, and keep unused slices upright on the bread rack. Once all inserted slices are correctly positioned, press the toaster lever fully down. Confirm that the bread remains in place and the lever stays depressed. Release all objects, fully open both grippers and return both arms to their initial poses. make_toast_random uses the same description.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** No bread slice is inserted into a toaster slot.
- **25 points:** One bread slice is inserted into a toaster slot and all bread axes satisfy the required orientation.
- **50 points:** Two bread slices are inserted into the toaster slots and all bread axes satisfy the required orientation.
- **100 points:** Both toaster slots are filled, exactly two bread slices remain on the shelf, the toaster control is pressed down, and the robot returns to origin.
