## Description

Assign each group of stacking-toy pieces to the appropriate peg according to the number of pieces in the group and the peg's length. Place each group on its corresponding peg so that the group size matches the length of the peg. Every piece must fit fully onto the peg and settle into place rather than leaning against its side, resting on its top, or remaining suspended above it. Check that each peg holds the correct number of fully seated pieces, including any pieces that are obscured or omitted. Once assembly is complete, fully open both grippers and return both arms to their initial poses.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** No peg group is completed.
- **10 points:** One peg group is completed with its pieces aligned on the correct peg and inserted into the base, with the gripper open.
- **30 points:** Two peg groups are completed.
- **60 points:** Three peg groups are completed.
- **100 points:** All four peg groups are completed and the robot returns to origin.
