## Description

Remove the coin from its holder and deposit it completely into the coin bank through the slot. Confirm that the coin itself is being handled and that it actually enters the opening rather than remaining above it, resting on an edge, or becoming stuck in the slot. If it has not fully entered, continue correcting its placement until the entire coin is inside the bank. Release the coin, then fully open both grippers and return both arms to their initial poses.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** The coin is not lifted.
- **20 points:** The coin is lifted at least 8 cm.
- **100 points:** The coin bounding box is inside the coin bank slot region, and the robot returns to origin.
