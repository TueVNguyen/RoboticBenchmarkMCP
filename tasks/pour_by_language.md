## Description

Read the current language instruction to identify the destination bowl for each bottle and the required order of the bottle-and-bowl pairs. Pour the liquid into the corresponding bowls in that order, without using the wrong container or skipping an unfinished pair. Complete each transfer, wait for the liquid to settle, and bring the bottle upright before starting the next pair. Once the entire instructed sequence is complete, fully open both grippers and return both arms to their initial poses.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** The first target pour is not completed.
- **20 points:** The liquid from the first specified bottle is poured into its specified bowl, and the other two liquids have not been poured into their bowls.
- **50 points:** The first two specified liquids are poured into their bowls, and the third liquid has not yet been poured.
- **100 points:** All three specified liquids are poured into their corresponding bowls.
