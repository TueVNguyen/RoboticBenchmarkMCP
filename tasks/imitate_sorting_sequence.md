## Description

Watch the demonstrator place its objects into its basket and remember the complete order. Remain still throughout the demonstration without starting your own manipulation; moving during the demonstration results in a score of 0. Record the observed object sequence in task notes so that the complete order remains available for reference even when earlier visual frames fall out of context. After the demonstrator finishes and returns, place your corresponding objects into your basket in the same order. Release each object before proceeding to the next, keep later objects outside the basket until their turn, and leave the demonstration objects unchanged. Once the complete sequence has been reproduced, fully open both grippers and return both arms to their initial poses.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** The first sequence step is not completed, or the policy robot moves before the opposite robot arm finishes.
- **5 points:** The first target object is placed into the policy basket, later target objects are still outside it, all demonstration objects remain in the demo basket, and the gripper is open.
- **15 points:** The first two target objects are placed in the correct sequence.
- **30 points:** The first three target objects are placed in the correct sequence.
- **50 points:** The first four target objects are placed in the correct sequence.
- **100 points:** All five target objects are placed in sequence, all demonstration objects remain in the demo basket, the gripper is open, and the robot returns to origin.
