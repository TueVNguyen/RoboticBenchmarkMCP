## Description

The conveyor moves continuously at a constant speed. Observe the first object that appears on the conveyor and remember its appearance without picking it up. Continue observing later objects until a target matching the first object appears, then pick up that later matching object. When planning the grasp, you may consider estimating the object's movement speed from your observations. Raise the target by more than 10 cm relative to its initial height. Match based on the actual appearance and objects currently present, avoiding the first object and any nonmatching object. Finish with the target clearly lifted off the conveyor and securely held. Returning both arms to their initial poses is not required.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** The matching target object is not lifted high enough.
- **100 points:** The matching target object is lifted at least 10 cm.
