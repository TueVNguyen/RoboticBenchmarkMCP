## Description

Identify each tool's shape and orientation, find its matching recess in the toolbox, and place every tool in its corresponding position. Each tool must lie flat and fully seated in its recess rather than resting on an edge, crossing over another tool, or protruding outside the box. Check for omitted, misplaced, or incompletely seated tools and correct them. Finish with every tool inside the box and below the rim, fully open both grippers and return both arms to their initial poses.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** No tool is placed into its matching toolbox slot.
- **25 points:** Any one tool is placed in its matching toolbox slot with the gripper open.
- **50 points:** Any two tools are placed into their matching toolbox slots.
- **75 points:** Any three tools are placed into their matching toolbox slots.
- **100 points:** All four tools are placed in their matching slots, covered by the toolbox, and the robot returns to origin.
