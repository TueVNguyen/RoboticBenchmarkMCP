## Description

Match the charger's prongs to the designated socket on the power strip and insert the charger completely. Keep the prongs matched to the socket, and correct any misalignment or stalled insertion rather than leaving the charger above the socket or leaning against the strip. Confirm that the charger is fully seated and remains correctly plugged in after release, then fully open both grippers and return both arms to their initial poses.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** The charger is not fully plugged into the socket.
- **100 points:** The charger is inside the socket, inserted to the required depth, upright, and the robot returns to origin.
