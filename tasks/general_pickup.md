## Description

Read the current language instruction, identify the designated target among the objects in the scene, and pick it up. Use the current instruction and actual objects rather than assuming a fixed target, and confirm that the object taken is the requested one rather than a nearby distractor. Lift the target clearly above its initial supporting surface and keep it under stable control. Raise the target by more than 10 cm relative to its initial height. Finish with the target still held in the gripper, without releasing it. Returning both arms to their initial poses is not required.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** The target object is not lifted high enough.
- **100 points:** The target object is lifted at least 10 cm.
