## Description

Nest all bowls together into one complete, upright, stable stack. Each bowl must fit properly into the bowl below it rather than merely balancing on a rim, hanging at an angle, or remaining upside down outside the stack. Check for stuck, misaligned, or omitted bowls and adjust the existing stack as needed until every bowl is included. Confirm that the complete stack remains stable after release. Fully open both grippers and return both arms to their initial poses. stack_bowls_random uses the nesting instructions above. For stack_bowls, the bottom bowl must sit flat on the tabletop.

## Scoring

- **0 points:** No two bowls are stacked correctly.
- **15 points:** Any two bowls are stacked upright and the gripper is open.
- **100 points:** All three bowls are stacked together, all bowls are upright, the bottom bowl is strictly and stably placed on the table, and the robot returns to origin.
